# Industry Terms and Core Technologies for ZacAi

## Key AI Concepts & Techniques

- **Zero-Shot and Few-Shot Learning**  
  ZacAi employs zero-shot learning, which enables the system to generalize to new tasks without prior specific training examples. Few-shot learning extends this by allowing rapid model adaptation with very limited data, key for real-world flexibility and scalability.

- **Hybrid AI Models**  
  Combines neural architectures (transformers, CNNs, RNNs) with symbolic AI (rule-based systems, logic programming) and evolutionary algorithms for robust, interpretable, and adaptable learning and reasoning.

- **Neuro-Symbolic AI**  
  A fusion of neural networks for perception and symbolic systems for reasoning and knowledge representation, vital for explainability and abstract concept manipulation.

- **Meta-Learning (Learning to Learn)**  
  ZacAi models continually improve their ability to quickly learn new tasks by adapting internal parameters and architectures based on previous experiences.

- **Reinforcement Learning (RL) with Evolutionary Strategies**  
  RL allows learning via trial and error with feedback from the environment, while evolutionary strategies optimize policies by simulating natural selection and mutation across generations.

- **Large Language Models (LLM) Integration**  
  Using advanced pretrained transformers as part of the system for language understanding, generation, and contextual knowledge retrieval.

- **Explainable AI (XAI)**  
  Techniques like SHAP, LIME, counterfactual explanations, and symbolic summaries to make AI decisions transparent and understandable for users and auditors.

- **Self-Supervised Learning**  
  Leveraging unlabeled data with tasks like masked prediction or contrastive learning to build robust feature representations without heavy annotation requirements.

- **Model Pruning and Quantization**  
  Optimization methods to reduce model size and runtime latency, crucial for deploying ZacAi on edge devices or constrained environments.

- **Continuous Learning and Online Adaptation**  
  Enabling models to update continuously with new data in deployment, preventing performance degradation over time (catastrophic forgetting).

## Architectural and Development Terms

- **Modular Microservices Architecture**  
  Structuring ZacAi as independent, scalable service components communicating over APIs for flexibility and ease of upgrade.

- **API-First Design with REST/GraphQL**  
  Facilitates seamless frontend-backend integration in Next.js and other frameworks.

- **State Management and Persistent Context**  
  Effective memory and state synchronization strategies to maintain long-term interaction coherence.

- **CI/CD Pipelines for AI/ML**  
  Automated testing, versioning, and deployment pipelines specific to ML models and data-driven workflows to ensure stability and rapid iteration.

- **Containerization and Orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes)**  
  Standardizing deployment environments and enabling scalable orchestration of ZacAi’s modular services.

- **Edge AI and Federated Learning**  
  Exploring decentralized model training and inference on devices closer to data sources for privacy and latency gains.
